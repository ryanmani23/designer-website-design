Privacy

Updated June 2026

What we collect

We collect only what you give us. When you fill out the contact form or request a material sample, we receive your name, email, phone, location, and any details you provide about your project. We do not use third-party analytics or advertising trackers on this site.

How we use it

We use your information to respond to your inquiry, schedule a consultation, and follow up on the work we discuss. We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties for marketing.

Where it lives

Form submissions are routed through Formspree, which forwards them to our team email. We retain records of customer correspondence for the duration of a project and a reasonable period afterward for warranty and reference purposes.

Your choices

To request a copy of, correct, or delete any information we hold about you, email designer@priorityroofs.com. We will respond within a reasonable timeframe.
